# Human review of a ContractNLI attempt

Review the exact retained input and output, not the room's current files.
Record case, workflow/checker revision and Attempt identifiers with feedback.

| Criterion | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| Classification | Does the whole contract support, contradict or omit the hypothesis? |
| Source fidelity | Do cited span IDs and quotations match the supplied contract? |
| Sufficiency | Do the cited passages establish the decision, including exceptions? |
| Completeness | Is material qualifying or contradictory text missing? |
| Reference quality | Is the published annotation defensible? Are other passages valid? |
| Uncertainty | Does the explanation acknowledge ambiguity without inventing facts? |

A reviewer may mark a criterion incorrect, correct, uncertain or not applicable,
and quote the exact supporting passage. Explain disagreements with reference
labels rather than overwriting the original score. A need for adjudication is
not a zero score. These are review instructions, not a functioning submission
form or a claim that general Assessment APIs have already shipped.

Independent legal expertise is needed before making a practical legal-quality
claim. Source annotations and exact match are baselines, not that expertise.
